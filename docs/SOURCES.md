# Sources

All sources are public. Formulas are standard; worked examples, sweep widths, the cueing model and the sensor value comparison are the author's.

## Search theory

- Koopman, B. O., *Search and Screening*, Operations Evaluation Group Report No. 56, US Navy, 1946.
- Koopman, B. O., *Search and Screening: General Principles with Historical Applications*, Pergamon, 1980.
- Stone, L. D., *Theory of Optimal Search*, 2nd ed., Operations Research Society of America, 1989.
- Washburn, A. R., *Search and Detection*, 5th ed., INFORMS, 2014.
- Frost, J. R., "Principles of Search Theory", Parts I to IV, *Response*, 1999 (sweep width, coverage factor and POD explained for SAR planners).
- International Maritime Organization and International Civil Aviation Organization, *IAMSAR Manual*, Volume II, *Mission Co-ordination* (sweep width, coverage factor, POD, probability of success, datum and search area expansion).

## Detection, false alarms and operator burden

- Swets, J. A., *Signal Detection Theory and ROC Analysis in Psychology and Diagnostics*, Lawrence Erlbaum, 1996.
- Richards, M. A., *Fundamentals of Radar Signal Processing*, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill, 2014 (Pd, Pfa, thresholding).
- Kahneman, D. and Tversky, A., "On the Psychology of Prediction", *Psychological Review* 80 (1973): 237-251 (base-rate neglect).
- Benaskeur, A., "Holonic Architectural Paradigm for Complex Modern C2", *Annals of Command and Control* 3(1), NATO Command and Control Centre of Excellence, 2026 (data saturation and cognitive overload from pervasive integration; research article, not NATO policy).

## Tracking and cueing

- Bar-Shalom, Y., Willett, P. K. and Tian, X., *Tracking and Data Fusion: A Handbook of Algorithms*, YBS Publishing, 2011.
- Blackman, S. and Popoli, R., *Design and Analysis of Modern Tracking Systems*, Artech House, 1999.
- Johnson, J., "Analysis of Image Forming Systems", *Proceedings of the Image Intensifier Symposium*, US Army Engineer Research and Development Laboratories, 1958 (detection, recognition and identification as resolution thresholds).

## Footprint geometry

- Driggers, R. G., Friedman, M. H. and Nichols, J. M., *Introduction to Infrared and Electro-Optical Systems*, 2nd ed., Artech House, 2012.

## What is not sourced

Every numeric example uses assumed sweep widths, speeds and alert rates chosen for teaching. The cueing time model (`isrsearch.cueing`) and the sensor value model (`isrsearch.burden.sensor_value`) are the author's analytical aids. No figure in this repository describes the measured performance of any product.
